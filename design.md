# Project Brief: CholooPujoo

## Project Overview
**CholooPujoo** is a premium, mobile-first web application designed to help users navigate and experience the Durga Puja festival in Kolkata. The app focuses on heritage, community, and modern festive discovery through a sleek, immersive interface.

## Target Audience
- **Cultural Explorers:** Users interested in the historical "Bonedi Bari" (family household) pujos.
- **Urban Adventurers:** Users navigating the high-traffic, modern "Sarbojanin" (community) pujos in North and South Kolkata.
- **Plan-Oriented Users:** People looking to build itineraries, check live crowd statuses, and share their plans.

## Design Philosophy (Espresso & Ember)
The visual identity is defined by the **Espresso & Ember** design system:
- **Palette:** Deep espresso brown backgrounds (#1f0f0d) paired with vibrant coral/orange-red accents (#FF4D3D) and off-white typography.
- **Aesthetic:** Minimalist, high-fidelity, and dark-themed to reflect the moody, warm atmosphere of oil lamps and festive nights.
- **Imagery:** Architectural and abstract textures only (heritage arches, vintage lanterns, LED installations). Strictly no human faces or portraits to maintain an immersive, atmospheric feel.

## Core Feature Set
1. **Authentication:** Secure entry with a festive, modern login experience.
2. **Discovery (Home):** Categorized access to "Bonedi Bari," "North Kolkata," and "South Kolkata" pujos with immersive visual cards.
3. **Pandal Navigation (List View):**
   - Detailed list of pandals within each category.
   - Live crowd indicators (Medium, High, Extreme).
   - Instant actions: Bookmark, Get Directions, and Mark as Completed.
4. **My Itinerary (Dashboard):**
   - Personal list of "Want to Go" vs "Completed" pandals.
   - Social sharing via a dedicated WhatsApp integration.
5. **Interactive Mapping:** (Planned) Visualizing pujo locations for efficient routing.

## User Flow
1. **Landing:** Authenticate via the "Festive Noir" sign-in screen.
2. **Explore:** Land on the Home screen to choose a pujo category.
3. **Select:** Browse specific pandals in a 1:1 consistent list layout.
4. **Action:** Bookmark a pujo or mark it as completed after visiting.
5. **Manage:** View and share the personalized itinerary from the Dashboard.
